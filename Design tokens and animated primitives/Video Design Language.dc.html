05Stationsstops on the canvas, not slides

Each station keeps the previous one alive on screen: the node you came from stays visible at the edge of frame, the edge you travelled stays drawn. A cut that clears the screen is the one move this system never makes.

06Camera & continuityone canvas · one continuous move

The single decision that stops it reading as a deck: there is one canvas per video, and it never resets. Every idea is a node placed in real space, every relationship is a drawn edge, and a "scene" is just where the camera is looking. A → B is a pan along the edge that connects them; the payoff is pulling back to reveal that A, B and C were one structure the whole time. Drive it below.

rules
01Move, never cut. 900ms pan or zoom on the expo curve. Hard cuts only at chapter boundaries.
02Nothing is destroyed. Visited nodes stay drawn and dim to muted. The canvas accumulates.
03Edge before node. The connector draws first, then its target enters — the viewer sees the relationship before the content.
04Zoom out to pay off. Every chapter ends on the wide shot of what was just built.
05Reels crop, they don't redesign. Vertical is a narrower camera on the same canvas.
