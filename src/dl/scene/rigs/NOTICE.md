# Iona art v1

Original procedural character and paper glider authored for aideos. MIT, under the repository LICENSE. Promoted from `test_fixtures/performance/iona/character.mjs`; geometry, face paint, cel ramp and outlines preserve the approved art. The production adapter owns pose and placement, and the host owns camera and lighting.

Reference authority is the frozen source hashes recorded in `registry.ts`, with the front, three-quarter, profile, silhouette and expression entry points in the archived fixture `character.mjs`, plus the approved catch evidence in `performance.mjs` and `filmstrip.mjs`. No per-film copy, model download or second-character retargeting is included.

Three.js is installed from npm at 0.180.0 (MIT); its licence is included by the package. The historical fixture's bundled Three is not used by the stage.
