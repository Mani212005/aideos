Usage: aideos-backend review [options] <target>

Review a rendered video against the good-video rubric: measurable checks on the
mp4 (and film data), evidence frames, exit 1 when a gate fails

Arguments:
  target                      a film slug under videos/ or the path of an mp4

Options:
  --format <format>           review long or reel; default reviews every
                              rendered format
  --film <film.json>          film data to read the camera, persistence and
                              geometry from (a slug review finds it itself)
  --words <file>              word timings (voiceover_words.json) or narration
                              text, for audio sync, caption matching and
                              grounding
  --out <dir>                 where review.json and the review/ evidence frames
                              are written
  --no-ocr                    skip the OCR pass (no caption, readability,
                              overlap or grounding checks)
  --pacing-profile <profile>  opt-in narration pace window for reel reviews:
                              reel-fast allows up to 240 words per minute
                              (default: 145 to 215)
  --json                      print the report as JSON on stdout (progress goes
                              to stderr)
  -h, --help                  display help for command
