# Iona proof provenance

`character.mjs`, `performance.mjs` and `dots.mjs` are unchanged source recovered from the
approved original Iona art proof on 2026-10-13. Iona is an original wind courier and the
paper glider is original procedural geometry; no third-party character mesh or texture
is included. These proof sources are covered by this repository's MIT license.

Recovery source: `aideos-character-art-proof/report.md`, final actual
`SCOUT_ARCHIVE_BASE64_BEGIN` marker, gzip tar SHA-256:
`e43d73fcc0a80e6b257e3c373d923633d6eb52aad6171f8ef0a5885ce783ac66`.
The diffusion architecture report carries identical character/performance source excerpts;
its recovery gzip tar SHA-256 is
`8517052983cea0e2d77a420ce8e91fc556783caf5c409ece9a9e444843021906`.
The original clip was approved as the bar with cel shading primary and dots optional.
Its historical board is ended and is not reopened by this fixture.

The original authoring used Claude Sonnet 5.5; historical model reviews used Gemini 3.8
Flash through agy. Restoration and deterministic evaluation require no model calls or keys.
Rigid mesh joints, limited deformation, painted facial features and finger-contact limits
remain properties of the accepted proof. This fixture does not claim to solve them.

`three.core.min.js` and `three.module.min.js` are unmodified Three.js 0.180.0 MIT distribution
files from that archive. Keep `three-LICENSE.txt` beside them. No fonts are redistributed.
`filmstrip.mjs` is the new isolated verification adapter, covered by the repository license.
The whole fixture is about 768 KiB, including Three.js, rather than storing reference movies.
