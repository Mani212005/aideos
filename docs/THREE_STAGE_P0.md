# Opt-in cel stage (P0)

The production path supports one or more characters, each with its own illustrative paper glider and receive, on a persistent orthographic stage. Characters are data: see `docs/CHARACTER_ENGINE.md` and `characters/README.md`; Iona is the approved library character. Author `aideos.look/2` and `aideos.design/2`, then continue the usual `runProduction` look and design stops. The worked data contract is `test_fixtures/packages/iona-receive/design/`; copy the contract into a new package through the package helpers, rather than committing personal films.

`scene.renderer: "three"` selects the stage once inside FilmView. Editor Player and Video export reach that same component. Legacy SVG films retain their existing path, and legacy `scene.actors` remain rejected. Compiled scenes keep the existing scene version with strict optional cast and performance fields; the authored look/design version identifies the new contract without migrating SVG films.

The decisive event names a shot, a spoken phrase, its start/end edge and `contact`. Resolve it against `voiceover_words.json` independently of the action. `bindReceiveSpan` shifts every timed action channel into film time, reserves at least one second of preparation and preserves 2.7 seconds of recovery. A late contact that cannot settle before narration ends fails the design. The film stores compact action data, never per-frame matrices. Stage captions store individual measured word cues so parsing cannot redistribute contact timing. Timing and frozen resource contents participate in production resume fingerprints.

`src/dl/scene/rigs/registry.ts` freezes the engine and reference hashes (re-freeze with `npx tsx scripts/freeze_stage_resources.ts`). The compiled cast embeds each character's verified spec; `src/dl/scene/characters/` builds its geometry, face paint, cel ramps and outlines and applies complete absolute poses. Iona's spec reproduces the frozen P0 module (`test_fixtures/characters/iona-legacy/`) bit for bit. Each context owns its resources. The glider stays in stage space through attachment rather than reparenting. Material or art edits require an explicit registry re-freeze. Provenance and licence notices live beside the asset. Dependency versions are exact in package.json; the adapter uses APIs available in Remotion 4.0.499.

`src/dl/scene/projection.ts` owns centered cover, viewport and orthographic camera projection for both runtime and checks. Three stage framing bypasses the outer shot drift, which would otherwise invalidate bounds. SVG ground and foreground planes are explicit; Iona and glider share a depth buffer. The frame barrier assigns absolute state, prepares shaders, draws and waits for GL completion before releasing Remotion capture and Player buffering. Obsolete async completions cannot release a newer frame. Missing bundled SVGs, incompatible rigs and context loss fail visibly instead of becoming placeholder evidence.

P0 checks measure numerical contact and conservative posed mesh bounds, including outline allowance, in both crops. Review facts name cast, action, independent cue, phase, ownership and after-state. They explicitly label raster visibility, depth occlusion, facial appeal and finger contact as unmeasured. The SVG-only vision sampler rejects Three scenes. The ordinary review rubric still reports the fixed camera's limitations; P0 is an integration exit, not acceptance of a finished explainer.

The original P0 slice was deliberately thin: one receive writer, Iona's identity bind basis, fixed camera evidence, conservative boxes rather than occlusion, minimal look adherence and stage facts. Data-driven characters and receive retargeting have since landed; see `docs/CHARACTER_ENGINE.md` and `characters/README.md`. Full stage gates/insets are documented in `docs/THREE_STAGE_P2.md`; look adherence and review truth for characters in `docs/THREE_STAGE_P3.md`; a 45-60 second explainer and dots remain later phases. General timeline retiming does not rebind authored stage actions; rebuild the design after narration/timing edits.

Run the root test/lint commands and smoke:studio sequentially with rendering: the fixture tests and production both own the generated active-film module. For hosts with a local Chrome, ProductionRequest.browserExecutable avoids a browser download. Short renders use renderConcurrency: 2. Preserve completed render files when rechecking, and check disk before rendering.
